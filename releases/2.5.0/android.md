Say less, agents!

In this Convos update, you get more control about when, where and whether agents participate:

+ LISTEN mode limits agents from chiming in unless you ask them to

+ PAUSE agents, and they can’t see anything in the groupchat, even after they’re unpaused

+ 1:1 AGENT CHAT is a sidebar where you can drop and discuss anything, without blowing up the groupchat

+ Speed and reliability improvements
